About Us

Yethy African Pantry

Bringing the true taste of Africa to your home.

Yethy African Pantry was born from a simple idea: that everyone — whether you're in Lagos, Abuja, or anywhere in the world — deserves access to the authentic taste of Africa.

We source our products carefully, working with trusted farmers and suppliers to bring you fresh, high-quality Nigerian foodstuffs. From garri and ponmo to egusi, crayfish, ofada rice and palm oil — every item is chosen to taste like home.

Whether you're cooking for your family, running a restaurant, or stocking up your pantry, we're here to make sure you never run out of the essentials.

Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

How do I place an order?

Browse our shop, add items to your cart, then complete checkout. Your order is sent to us via WhatsApp where we confirm the final details.

Do you deliver nationwide?

Yes — we deliver across Nigeria. Delivery fees vary by location and are confirmed on WhatsApp before payment.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer and cash on delivery (for select areas). Payment details are shared via WhatsApp when you place your order.

Do you offer wholesale / bulk pricing?

Yes! Many of our products are available in wholesale quantities. Contact us on WhatsApp for a custom quote.

How fresh are your products?

We source fresh stock regularly and package everything with care to preserve quality and taste.

Can I return an item?

If there is an issue with your order, please reach out within 24 hours of delivery and we will make it right.
