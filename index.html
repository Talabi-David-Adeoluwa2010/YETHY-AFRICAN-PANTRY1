— About —

Yethy African Pantry

Bringing the true taste of Africa to your home.

Yethy African Pantry began in a small kitchen in East London, with a simple frustration: finding authentic Nigerian foodstuffs — the kind that tastes like your mother's cooking — was nearly impossible without a trip to a crowded market or a relative's suitcase.

So we built the pantry we wished existed. Every product is sourced directly from trusted farmers and producers across Nigeria, carefully packaged, and shipped to your door within days — not weeks.

Whether you're cooking for your family, running a restaurant, or stocking up for the winter — we're here so you never have to compromise on taste.

Shipping Policy

Everything you need to know about how we deliver your order.

Dispatch & Delivery Times

All orders are packed and dispatched from our warehouse in London, United Kingdom. Once dispatched, delivery times vary by destination:

  • United Kingdom: 3 business days (fully tracked)
  • Europe & North America: 7–10 business days
  • Rest of World: 7–10 business days

Orders placed before 2:00 PM GMT on a weekday are typically dispatched the same day. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are dispatched the next business day.

Shipping Costs

  • UK Standard: £4.95 — free on orders over £40
  • UK Express: £8.95 (1–2 business days)
  • International: Calculated at checkout based on destination and weight
  • Wholesale / bulk: Custom quote provided via WhatsApp

Tracking

You will receive a tracking number via email (and WhatsApp, if opted in) as soon as your parcel leaves our warehouse. Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours to appear in the courier's system.

Customs & Import Duties

For international orders, any import duties, taxes, or customs fees levied by the destination country are the responsibility of the customer. Yethy African Pantry is not liable for delays caused by customs processing.

Delivery Issues

If your parcel is delayed beyond the estimated timeframe, or arrives damaged, please contact us within 7 days of the expected delivery date. We'll investigate with the courier and arrange a replacement or refund as appropriate.

Note: Delivery timeframes are estimates provided by our courier partners and are not guaranteed. Delays can occur during peak periods (e.g., Christmas, Ramadan, summer holidays).

Contact

For any shipping-related questions, message us on WhatsApp or email hello@yethyafricanpantry.com. We aim to respond within 24 hours.

Refund Policy

Our promise if something isn't right with your order.

Our Promise

We want every order to arrive fresh, complete, and exactly as described. If for any reason it doesn't, we'll make it right — quickly and fairly.

Returns & Refunds — 14 Days

You may request a return or refund within 14 days of delivery if:

  • Your order arrived damaged, spoiled, or leaking
  • You received the wrong item(s)
  • Items are missing from your order
  • The product is significantly different from what was described

Items Not Eligible for Refund

  • Perishable or opened food items — for hygiene and safety reasons
  • Custom or wholesale orders — subject to separate terms
  • Products damaged due to misuse or improper storage after delivery
  • Requests made after the 14-day window

How to Request a Refund

Use the Request a Refund button in the top dashboard, or email us with:

  • Your order reference (from your WhatsApp confirmation)
  • Photographs of the issue (damaged / incorrect item)
  • A short description of what went wrong

We aim to review every request within 2 business days.

Refund Method & Timing

  • Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method
  • Please allow 5–10 business days for the refund to appear
  • In some cases we may offer a replacement or store credit instead — you choose

Return Shipping

If the error was on our side (wrong item, damaged goods), we cover the return shipping cost. If you simply changed your mind, return shipping is the customer's responsibility, and items must be unopened and in original packaging.

Perishables: Because our products are food items, we may ask for photographs of spoiled or damaged goods rather than requesting a physical return. This helps us resolve your refund faster.

Contact

Questions? WhatsApp us anytime or email hello@yethyafricanpantry.com.
